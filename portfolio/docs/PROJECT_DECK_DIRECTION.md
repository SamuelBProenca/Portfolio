# Project deck experiment

Scope: an extension of the established portfolio, on `/experimentos/cards`. Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters, technical peers and prospective clients. The owner confirmed updated home copy plus a separate experimental deck; the accordion remains the default. This is a code-led interaction prototype informed by the supplied HTML/image, not a replacement visual identity or a pixel-match request.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make four real projects easy to inspect as substantial cards, with an active card and restrained desktop neighbor previews. Complete reading and predictable controls take precedence over reproducing the supplied mobile crop.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the existing canvas/paper/terracotta tokens, display/body faces, hairlines, portrait and authentic project material. No new brand palette, generated portraits, client logos or invented metrics.

STORY: Identify the project and maturity, read a useful summary, inspect its evidence and follow a real link. GreatMaker leads this experiment; Curriculum Studio, OctoCards and LinkKeeper follow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A short experiment introduction and comparison link lead into one fully visible card. On narrow screens the card fits inside the gutters; neighboring cards do not cover its content. Previous/next and direct selection controls remain visible and at least 44 px.

FORM: A progressively enhanced semantic project list. All four projects and links exist in server HTML without JavaScript. Enhancement provides bounded selection, restrained desktop stacking, keyboard access, then deliberate horizontal gestures and deferred Motion. No autoplay or free rearrangement. Existing-world extension; no concept roll applies.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Verification boundaries

The default home, seven-item GitHub-backed catalog, contact, Curriculum proxy and OctoCards plan stay independent. Motion belongs only to the experiment, is imported after interaction intent, and is not required to read or select cards. Reduced motion snaps; failed animation code preserves controls. Nested CTA clicks and drags are tested separately. Public Curriculum GET was checked; public form/PDF was not retested.

No shipping raster is added or replaced: the portrait and GreatMaker screenshot retain the provenance recorded in `PROJECT_SOURCES.md`. Review captures are evidence of local rendering, not product screenshots or client results. The supplied mobile comp remains a critique reference; its cropping is explicitly rejected.
